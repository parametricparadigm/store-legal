Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

This Privacy Policy explains how this store ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our e-commerce operations and our integrations with third-party marketplaces such as eBay, Etsy, and Shopify.

Information we collect

We process order and account information needed to fulfill purchases: names, shipping addresses, contact details, order contents, and transaction identifiers. This data is provided by the marketplaces through their official APIs when you place an order.

How we use information

We use it solely to process, ship, and support your orders, to communicate about those orders, and to meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

We share information only with service providers required to fulfill orders (e.g. marketplaces, payment processors, shipping carriers and print/dropship suppliers), and only as needed to complete your purchase.

Data retention

We retain order records for as long as required to provide service and comply with legal, accounting, and reporting requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements. Marketplace account-deletion requests are honored through the marketplace's official process.
